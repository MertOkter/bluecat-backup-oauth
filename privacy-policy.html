Privacy Policy

BlueCat Backup is a private application used by its owner for backup and recovery.

The application accesses Google Drive only to create, read, list, and manage backup files authorized by the account owner.

Google Drive data is used only to perform backup, verification, and recovery operations requested or configured by the account owner.

OAuth access tokens used by rclone are stored locally on the owner's device for authentication. This website does not collect or store Google account passwords, OAuth tokens, or backup file contents.

Backup files are stored in the account owner's Google Drive. Data is not sold or shared with third parties, except for storage and processing by Google Drive as required to provide the backup service.

Backup files remain in Google Drive until the account owner deletes them. Google Drive access may be revoked at any time through the owner's Google Account permissions.

This application is intended for personal use only.
